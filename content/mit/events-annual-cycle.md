The year at MIT, event by event. (2026–27 dates verified where shown; the rest recur annually — calendar.mit.edu is the live institute calendar.)

## Fall 2026
- **REX**: Aug 29 – Sep 2 · **Convocation**: Aug 30 · **Activities Midway** (club fair, Johnson): orientation week
- **Panhel recruitment**: Sep 4–8 · **IFC rush kickoff**: Sep 5 (bids Sep 11)
- **HackMIT**: Sep 19–20 (~1,200 hackers)
- **EECS Career Fair**: Sep 24 · **Fall Career Fair**: Sep 25, Johnson Athletic Center (employer interviews Sep 26; register on Handshake in August)
- **Family Weekend**: Oct 23–24
- **Head of the Charles** regatta (Oct) fills the river with spectators
- **Splash** (ESP's giant teach-anything weekend for high schoolers, taught by students): **Nov 21–22, 2026**
- Halloween: pumpkin drop, haunted dorm events; **piano drop** at Baker marks fall Drop Date (Nov 18)
- **Tech Twinkles** (holiday lights + performances at the Student Center): early December

## IAP / January 2027
- **Mystery Hunt**: MLK weekend, Jan 16–18, 2027 (Team Providence writes it; the 2026 hunt drew ~280 teams and 5,000+ solvers)
- **Battlecode / Pokerbots / web.lab / MASLAB** finals, **StartMIT**, micro-internships
- MLK Day celebration events: Jan 18

## Spring 2027
- **xFair** (TechX's student-run career fair + tech expo, 2,000+ attendees): typically early February
- **getfit** fitness challenge: Feb–Apr (2026 ran Feb 9 – Apr 5)
- **Ring Premiere** (sophomore ring unveiling, Kresge) in late winter; **Ring Delivery** (junior-year formal at a Boston venue) in early May — e.g. the '27s premiered Feb 16 and got rings May 4
- **Blueprint** (HackMIT's high-school hackathon): late Feb/early Mar · **Spark** (ESP, for middle schoolers): mid-March
- **Spring break**: late March · **CPW** (admitted students): April (2026 was Apr 16–19)
- **SpringFest** (SEB's carnival + concert — 2026: May 1–2, Ravyn Lenae headlining Johnson Ice Rink)
- **delta v Demo Day** happens each September (Kresge) for the summer accelerator cohort; the **MIT $100K** finale is each May

## Commencement week (May 26–28, 2027)
OneMIT ceremony, undergraduate ceremony, advanced-degree ceremonies and hooding in Killian Court; senior week events precede it; graduates flip their Brass Rats.

## All year
- **LSC (Lecture Series Committee)**: $5 movies in 26-100 (26-foot screen, 35mm + digital, since 1944)
- **MIT Museum** (free with ID + one guest; monthly 18+/21+ **After Dark** nights are $10 with MIT ID) and **List Center** programming
- **Muddy Charles Pub** (grad pub in Walker, weekday evenings)
- Dorm events via dormspam — subscribe and filter (see the computing topic)
- The live feed of everything: calendar.mit.edu and engage.mit.edu/events
