MIT runs on a 4-1-4 calendar: fall term, IAP, spring term, plus optional summer.

## Fall term (early September – mid December)
- **Registration Day** just before classes start.
- Classes begin the Wednesday after Labor Day (approximately).
- **Add Date** (~5 weeks in): last day to add subjects.
- **Drop Date** (~11 weeks in): last day to drop without record or change grading options.
- Thanksgiving break, then last week of classes, reading period, and **finals week** ending around Dec 20.

## IAP — Independent Activities Period (January)
A four-week January term unique to MIT. Optional for most students (some departments have required IAP subjects). People use it for:
- Short for-credit classes (e.g., 18.02A completion, 6.S-series specials)
- Non-credit activities: web.lab, pokerbots, Battlecode, MASLAB robotics
- **Mystery Hunt** (the giant puzzle competition over MLK weekend)
- **Externships** (month-long placements with alumni companies, via CAPD — apply in the fall)
- UROP research, ski trips, or just going home

## Spring term (early February – late May)
Same structure: Add Date, Drop Date, spring break (late March), finals ending late May, then **Commencement**.

## Summer
No regular undergrad term; most students do internships, UROPs, or MISTI abroad. A small number of subjects are offered.

## Key deadline concepts
- **Pre-registration** happens online before each term (including IAP); adjusting during the term is normal.
- **CPW (Campus Preview Weekend)** — April weekend for admitted prospective first-years.
- **Registration holds** (medical forms, bills) block registration — clear them early.

Exact dates move every year — always check the official Registrar calendar at registrar.mit.edu/calendar.
