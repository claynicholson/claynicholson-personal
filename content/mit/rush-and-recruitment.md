MIT has ~44 FSILGs across four councils: the IFC (~25 fraternities), Panhellenic (6 sororities), the Multicultural Greek Council (8 culturally based organizations), and the Living Group Council (independent living groups). Roughly a third of undergrads affiliate. (2026 dates verified Aug 2026.)

## The one rule that shapes everything
**First-years must live on campus and cannot live in an FSILG.** You rush and join as a first-year but move into the house sophomore year at the earliest — so year one is about the people, not the bed.

## IFC fraternity rush — Fall 2026
- Rush calendar posts ~Sep 4; **Kresge Kickoff Sat Sep 5, 12pm** on Kresge lawn — the first official event, all ~25 chapters present.
- A week of open events (house dinners, Cape Cod and Six Flags trips, tours) that turn **invite-only** later in the week; no events during class hours once the term starts.
- **Bids from Fri Sep 11**; responses accepted starting Sep 12, and bids stay open several days.
- Missing fall isn't fatal: a spring bid window runs roughly February to mid-May, and several houses recruit informally year-round.
- Money: IFC-average new-member fees are a few hundred dollars for the fall; living in later averages ~$5,900/semester all-in (usually at or below dorm + meal plan; financial aid applies). Costs vary a lot by house — ask each one directly.
- Standing: MIT publishes a hazing transparency report (hazefree.mit.edu); as of mid-2026 no chapters were on violation status.

## Panhellenic sorority recruitment — Fall 2026
- **Fully Structured Recruitment: Sep 4–8, 2026** (the week before classes): four days of themed rounds with each of the six chapters — Alpha Chi Omega, Alpha Phi, Delta Phi Epsilon, Kappa Alpha Theta, Pi Beta Phi, Sigma Kappa — ending in preference and Bid Day.
- Register in advance at mitpanhel.com/recruitment.
- **COB (Continuous Open Bidding)** runs informally after FSR and in spring for chapters below capacity.

## ILGs (Living Group Council)
Six independent living groups: **Epsilon Theta** (Brookline), **Fenway House**, **pika** (co-op), **MIT Student House**, **tEp** (Tau Epsilon Phi), and **WILG** (women's group). Four of five residential ILGs are gender-inclusive; they run 20–40 people and are typically cheaper than dorms. Student House, Fenway, and pika recruit informally all semester; ET and WILG hold events during rush week and again early spring (WILG's spring 2026 rush was Feb 3–8). They are not on the IFC calendar — you have to go find them.

## MGC and NPHC organizations
The eight Multicultural Greek Council organizations (historically Black and Latinx fraternities/sororities) conduct **intake year-round**, not on the fall calendar — reach out to chapters directly or via the FSILG Office.

## Non-Greek "rush" happening the same weeks
- **Dorm culture**: REX events and in-dorm room assignment (see the orientation topic)
- **A cappella and ensembles**: auditions during orientation/September (acapella.mit.edu; ensembles via 21M)
- **Varsity walk-ons**: contact head coaches directly (mitathletics.com staff directory)
- **Club deadlines**: many application-based clubs recruit in September — watch dormspam and the Activities Midway
