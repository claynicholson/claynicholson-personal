The 2026 cycle, verified against firstyear.mit.edu and housing.mit.edu (Aug 2026). The pattern repeats yearly: info posts ~May 1, FPOP apps due early June, detailed schedule via the Guidebook app mid-August, FPOPs the week before Orientation, classes the Wednesday after Labor Day.

## Pre-arrival deadlines (summer 2026 cycle)
- Housing application: **June 3, 12pm** (same day FPOP applications close, 5pm; they open May 13)
- **FEE (First-Year Essay Evaluation)**: June 8–15 window (essays due 1pm June 15); determines whether your first CI-H must be a CI-HW
- Math diagnostic/self-assessment: mid-June window
- Final transcripts to Admissions: July 1
- Summer Modules Part 1: July 7–31 (Part 2 runs late Sept–Oct)
- Emergency Contact form on WebSIS: Aug 1
- Medical Report + immunization records: by ~Sep 1 — missing it puts a **hold on registration**

## Arrival and move-in (2026 dates)
- International students: arrive Fri Aug 28 (housing opened Aug 22); **iOrientation (mandatory, ISO-run) begins Sat Aug 29**
- Transfer students: move in Mon Aug 24
- FPOP participants: arrive Tue Aug 25 by 4pm
- First-year Move Day: **Sat Aug 29** (check-in desks 8am–midnight)
- Returning students: Fri Sep 4

## FPOPs (Freshman Pre-Orientation Programs)
20+ optional programs run Aug 25–29 by departments and offices — a great way to make early friends. Examples: Discover Mechanical Engineering, Discover Aerospace, Discover Product Design, DEAPS (Earth/Atmospheric/Planetary, incl. a Yellowstone trip), Aerospace in XR, FLIPOP (first-gen/low-income). Many are free; most charge a small housing fee. Apply mid-May.

## Orientation week (Aug 29 – Sep 8, 2026)
- **President's Convocation**: Sun Aug 30
- Advising meetings, department open houses, the swim test (offered on Registration Day), and the **Activities Midway** club fair in the Johnson Athletic Center
- **Parent & Family Orientation**: Aug 29–30
- **REX (Residential Exploration)**: Sat Aug 29 – Wed Sep 2 — dorm events so you can feel out cultures (schedule at dormcon.mit.edu/rex)
- **FYRE (First-Year Residence Exchange)**: the dorm-switch application, open Aug 29 12pm – Sep 1/2 (results by 5pm Sep 2); a new assignment is binding and you move on FYRE Move Day (~Sep 3)
- In-dorm room assignment follows, run by each dorm's student Room Assignment Chairs
- Registration Day Tue Sep 8; **classes start Wed Sep 9**

## Right after classes start
Sorority recruitment (Sep 4–8), fraternity/ILG rush from Sep 5 (see the rush topic), club auditions and tryouts, and the first psets. Advising continues — you must meet your advisor to finalize registration.
