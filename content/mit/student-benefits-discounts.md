The master list of what your MIT ID and Kerberos actually get you. (Verified Aug 2026; OGE also keeps an official index of free/low-cost resources.)

## Software (all free via IS&T)
Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, **Adobe Creative Cloud**, MATLAB (personal machines included), Mathematica, **SolidWorks**, Dropbox, Zoom, Slack, PyMOL, SnapGene, and more — the full grid is at ist.mit.edu/software-hardware. MIT also runs GitHub Enterprise (github.mit.edu), and the GitHub Student Developer Pack works with student status.

## News and media (via MIT Libraries)
Free access to the **New York Times, Boston Globe, The Atlantic, The Economist**, and other news sources, plus streaming (Kanopy) and the enormous journal/database collection — off campus via Touchstone or VPN.

## Museums and arts
- **MIT Museum**: free with MIT ID, plus one guest. **List Visual Arts Center**: free for everyone.
- **CAMIT Arts Access** (arts.mit.edu): free or subsidized student tickets to the **MFA, ICA, Isabella Stewart Gardner**, and the **BSO** ($5 performances via the Bobko BSO Student Pass).
- **Emerson/Harris program**: subsidized private music lessons for accomplished student musicians.

## MITAC (discounted tickets for the MIT community)
Ticket office in the Building 32 lobby (Tue–Fri 12–4) and online: discounted **AMC/Showcase/Landmark movie tickets, Red Sox and other pro sports, Canobie Lake Park, theater**, museums, and family outings.

## Transit
- **50% T-pass subsidy** for all enrolled students (70% for some grads) — order through Atlas by the 10th of the prior month.
- **Bluebikes $35/year** (vs ~$100+). Zipcar student discount. Free campus shuttles and grocery shuttles.

## Academics and advice
- **Free tutoring everywhere**: TSR² (16-159, open 8am–10pm daily — pset nights, exam reviews, 1-on-1), departmental tutoring for every science-core subject, HKN for EECS.
- **OCW and MITx**: free course materials for thousands of MIT subjects.
- Librarian research consultations, citation/data workshops, equipment lending at the libraries.

## Money, legal, admin
- **Free legal advice**: the BU/MIT Student Innovations Law Clinic (SILC) — confidential help with IP/media, privacy, and venture/finance matters for enrolled students; on-campus office hours.
- **Free notary**: Atlas Service Center (E17-106), 10am–3pm.
- **Sprintax** free federal tax filing for international students.
- Free B&W/color printing allowance (~$300/year via Pharos), free poster help via CopyTech pricing.

## Health and fitness
MIT Health visits, labs, and X-rays free for students regardless of insurance; free confidential STI testing; free mental health counseling. Z Center gym/pool access with student status; getfit fitness challenge each spring.

## Food safety-net
SwipeShare (donated meal swipes), TechMart (at-cost groceries in Walker), dorm pantries — see doingwell.mit.edu/foodandfinancial.
