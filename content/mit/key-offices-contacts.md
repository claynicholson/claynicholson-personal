The "who do I even email" cheat sheet. All contact info here is public; check each office's site for current hours/rooms.

## Academics
- **Registrar** (registrar.mit.edu, room 5-117) — registration, transcripts, calendars, degree audits, classroom issues.
- **Office of the First Year** (firstyear.mit.edu, room 11-101; Mon–Thu 9–5, Fri 9–3; 617-253-6771) — first-year advising, ASEs, transfer credit.
- **Committee on Curricula** (curricula@mit.edu) — petitions on GIR/degree-requirement exceptions; needs your advisor's signature.
- **Math Academic Services** (2-110) — math placement, transfer credit, ASE administration for Course 18 subjects.
- **CAP (Committee on Academic Performance)** — petitions (late drops, readmission); route through S³.
- Your **department's undergrad office** ("Course X undergrad office") — major requirements, department-specific everything; the academic administrators there are the most useful humans at MIT.

## Money
- **SFS (Student Financial Services)** (sfs.mit.edu, room 11-120) — aid, billing, student loans, employment.

## Life and support
- **S³ (Student Support Services)** (room 5-104; s3-support@mit.edu; 617-253-4861) — the front door for "life happened"; virtual drop-ins weekdays 10–12 and 2–4.
- **DAS (Disability & Access Services)** (also 5-104; das-student@mit.edu; 617-253-1674) — accommodations.
- **MIT Health** (medical.mit.edu, E23) — health, insurance, counseling; 24/7 line 617-253-4481.
- **Housing & Residential Services** — dorm assignments, maintenance requests.
- **DSL (Division of Student Life)** — the umbrella over housing/dining/activities.
- **ISO** (iso.mit.edu, E18-219) — international students.
- **OGE** (oge.mit.edu) — graduate education, GradSupport.
- **VPR (Violence Prevention & Response)** — confidential support, 24/7 line 617-253-2300.
- **Ombuds Office** — confidential conflict guidance.
- **CAPD** (capd.mit.edu, 7-103 and E17-294) — careers, pre-health/pre-law.

## Operations
- **IS&T Service Desk** (ist.mit.edu; Atlas Service Center, E17-106; 617-253-1101) — accounts, wifi, software.
- **Facilities** (via Atlas app/online) — report broken anything (dorm work orders).
- **MIT Police** — 617-253-1212, 24/7 (they also do free walk-home escorts).
- **Parking & Transportation** — T passes, shuttles, permits.
- **Card Services / MIT ID** — lost IDs, TechCASH.

## Getting things done: the meta-advice
Front-line staff and academic administrators fix 90% of problems if you email early, politely, with your ID number and specifics. For anything cross-office or stuck, S³ (undergrads) or GradSupport (grads) will quarterback it. Deans respond to respectful directness; MIT's bureaucracy is small and human by university standards.
