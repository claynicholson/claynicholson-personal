Housing at MIT is famously culture-driven: dorms have strong, distinct personalities, and students pick where they live.

## The undergrad dorms (west to east, roughly)
- **New House** — cultural houses (Chocolate City, La Casa, French House, German House, iHouse) + regular halls
- **Next House** — big, social, west-campus community
- **MacGregor** — singles for everyone, entry-based culture
- **Burton Conner** — floor-based cook-for-yourself culture
- **New Vassar** — newest dorm (2021), maker spaces, dining hall
- **Simmons Hall** — the "sponge" building, architectural landmark
- **Baker House** — Alvar Aalto building, dining hall, social
- **McCormick Hall** — all-women, dining hall
- **Maseeh Hall** — largest dorm, closest to classes, dining hall
- **Random Hall** — small, quirky, east-campus-spirit near central
- **East Campus** — DIY culture, courtyard builds during REX, cook-for-yourself

Dining-hall dorms require a meal plan; "cook-for-yourself" dorms (EC, Random, Burton Conner, MacGregor, New House in part) have kitchens and no required plan — this meaningfully affects cost and lifestyle.

## How you get housed
- Incoming first-years rank dorms over the summer (informed by videos/"i3" and guide-to-residences content) and get a summer assignment.
- **REX (Residence Exploration)** during orientation: dorms throw events so you can feel out cultures; afterwards a **First-Year Housing Adjustment lottery** lets you switch if you want.
- Within-dorm room assignment is run by each dorm (often very student-controlled: floor/entry pick their people).
- Returning students: in-dorm retention lottery first, cross-dorm transfer lottery if switching. Undergrads are guaranteed 4 years of housing; most live on campus all four.

## FSILGs (Fraternities, Sororities, and Independent Living Groups)
~35+ FSILGs, many with houses in Boston/Cambridge. First-years live in dorms (first-year-on-campus rule) but can rush/join immediately; members typically move in sophomore year. ILGs (co-ed independent houses like tEp, pika, Student House, WILG) are a distinct MIT institution — quirky, self-governed, often cheaper. Rush happens early fall; sorority recruitment (Panhel) is formal, fraternity/ILG rush is a free-food-events sprint.

## Grad housing (for reference)
Grad dorms include Sidney-Pacific, Ashdown, Tang, Westgate, Edgerton, Warehouse, Graduate Tower; separate application system, not guaranteed.
