## MISTI (MIT International Science & Technology Initiatives)
Fully-funded international internships, research, and teaching placements — MIT covers travel/living costs. Country programs (MISTI France, China, Israel, Germany, Japan, etc.) each with their own prep requirements (sometimes language classes). Apply the term before; summer is most popular. One of MIT's best-kept "actually free" deals.

## PKG Public Service Center
Service-oriented fellowships, IAP service programs, funding for public-interest internships and social-impact projects.

## D-Lab
Development-focused classes and fieldwork (design for affordability in developing communities), often with IAP/summer travel built into classes.

## Entrepreneurship
- **Martin Trust Center for MIT Entrepreneurship** — delta v accelerator (summer, funded), classes (15.390 New Enterprises), mentoring.
- **MIT Sandbox** — up to ~$25k in innovation funding + mentorship for any student idea (rolling applications; low barrier).
- **MIT $100K Competition** and **IDEAs Social Innovation Challenge** — pitch competitions with real prize money.
- **The Engine / VMS (Venture Mentoring Service)** — later-stage support.

## Other notable programs
- **Concourse / ESG / Terrascope / Media Arts** first-year learning communities — alternative ways to take the first-year core in small cohorts (apply the summer before).
- **NEET (New Engineering Education Transformation)** — cross-departmental project threads (autonomous machines, living machines).
- **ROTC** (Army/Navy/Air Force) hosted at MIT for cross-town students too.
- **Cross-registration**: MIT students can take classes at **Harvard** and **Wellesley** for credit (free, via cross-registration forms); Wellesley exchange includes a shuttle bus.
- **EuroTech / GTL (Global Teaching Labs)** — IAP teaching abroad via MISTI.
- **IROP** — international research placements.
