## MISTI (MIT International Science & Technology Initiatives)
Fully-funded international internships, research, and teaching placements in **40+ countries** — airfare, visa, accommodation, and a stipend are covered, and financial aid travels with you for study abroad. Open to all students in every major. Country programs each have their own prep requirements (sometimes language classes). Apply the term before; summer is most popular. One of MIT's best-kept "actually free" deals.

## PKG Public Service Center
Service-oriented fellowships, IAP service programs, funding for public-interest internships and social-impact projects.

## D-Lab
Development-focused classes and fieldwork (design for affordability in developing communities), often with IAP/summer travel built into classes.

## Entrepreneurship
- **Martin Trust Center for MIT Entrepreneurship** — the **delta v** summer accelerator (funded; Demo Day at Kresge each September), classes (15.390 New Enterprises), mentoring.
- **MIT Sandbox** — equity-free seed grants from **$1,000 up to $25,000**, three cohorts a year (spring/summer/fall), plus mentoring; the $1–5k tier is genuinely low-barrier for validating an idea.
- **MIT $100K Competition** and **IDEAs Social Innovation Challenge** — pitch competitions with real prize money.
- **The Engine / VMS (Venture Mentoring Service)** — later-stage support.

## Other notable programs
- **Concourse (~50 students), ESG (~55), Terrascope, and DesignPlus** — first-year learning communities: small-cohort alternatives for the first-year core (apply the summer before).
- **NEET (New Engineering Education Transformation)** — cross-departmental project threads.
- **ROTC** (Army/Navy/Air Force) hosted at MIT for cross-town students too.
- **Cross-registration**: full-time MIT students can take classes at **Harvard** (sophomores and up), **Wellesley**, and **MassArt** for credit at no extra tuition — generally up to half your term load; Wellesley runs an exchange bus. Wellesley classes can't fill science-core GIRs but count as electives/departmental requirements.
- **GTL (Global Teaching Labs)** — IAP teaching abroad via MISTI.
