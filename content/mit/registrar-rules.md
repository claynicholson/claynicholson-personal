The rules students actually hit, verified against registrar.mit.edu and catalog.mit.edu (Aug 2026).

## What an SB degree requires
- The **17 GIR subjects** (6 science core + 8 HASS + 2 REST + the 12-unit Lab), plus **180 units beyond the GIRs** from your degree chart. Up to six subjects (72 units) can double-count between major and GIRs, but those units don't count toward the 180.
- **Residency**: at least three regular terms at MIT, normally including your final term.
- Your **degree audit** in WebSIS tracks all of it automatically.

## Double majors and minors
- **Double major**: petition after ≥3 terms with a declared major; the stated bar is a **4.0+/5.0 GPA** (lower considered case-by-case with a letter). You complete both departments' full requirements including two CI-Ms in each. Same-area combos (like 2 and 2-A) aren't allowed. One degree, two majors.
- **Minors**: 5–7 subjects (typically six, ≥57 units); **max two minors**, awarded only with your SB. Seventeen interdisciplinary minors exist (Statistics & Data Science, Energy Studies, Entrepreneurship & Innovation, Astronomy, Biomedical Engineering, Environment & Sustainability, Public Policy, AADS, WGS...) plus departmental minors in most departments.
- Declaring: majors by spring Drop Date of first year (effective that summer; changeable anytime via a form — not binding); minors via the minor form with the minor advisor.

## Final exams
- Blocks are 9–12 and 1:30–4:30 during finals week. **Conflict exams** are scheduled by the Registrar only when you have two finals in the same block; you're notified after Drop Date.
- The schedule is built to minimize conflicts and multiple-exams-per-day; once published it cannot be changed or canceled. Searchable at finalexams.mit.edu.
- Ill or in crisis during finals? Go through **S³** — they handle excused absences and coordinate makeups/incompletes.

## Grades and records
- Grades are final except error corrections (fixable while enrolled or within 3 months of leaving) and I/O/OX resolution. An **Incomplete** must be finished by Add Date of your next regular term unless a later deadline is set.
- **Transcripts**: $10 via Parchment (electronic ~30 min, paper ~2 business days); records@mit.edu. Your internal **grade report** (WebSIS) shows +/- modifiers and term GPA; the external transcript doesn't.
- **FERPA**: MIT's directory shows name, course, email, and year by default; suppress it with the Directory Information form (note full suppression also blocks enrollment verification until you consent).

## Fees and petitions
- Late registration or late add/drop petitions: **$50**. After Add/Drop Dates everything is by petition (CAP for undergrads — route through S³).
- **Leave of absence**: arranged through an S³ dean (personal, medical, military); S³ also runs the return process.

## Cross-registration
Full-time students can cross-register at **Harvard (sophomores and up), Wellesley, and MassArt** — no extra tuition, generally up to half your term load; Wellesley subjects can't fill science-core GIRs. The earlier of the two schools' deadlines applies.
