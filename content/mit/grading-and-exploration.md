MIT's grading system is deliberately forgiving early on so students can adjust. (Specifics below checked against the Registrar's site, Aug 2026.)

## First year: Pass/No Record and ABC/No Record
- **Fall of first year AND IAP: Pass/No Record (P/NR).** Every subject is graded P (A/B/C-level work) or NR. An NR never appears on your external transcript — the subject simply vanishes. This is the famous "P/NR semester."
- **Spring of first year: ABC/No Record.** Grades of A, B, C appear on your transcript; D or F becomes NR (no external record). So you can take risks all year without a failing grade following you.
- Internally, hidden grades (like DN/FN notations) exist on internal records for advising, but never on the external transcript.

## First-year credit limits
Set by the Committee on Academic Performance (CAP), PE excluded:
- **Fall: 54 units** (+ up to 6 units of discovery-focused subjects)
- **IAP: 12 units** (applies to all students, not just first-years)
- **Spring: 60 units** (+ up to 6 units of discovery subjects)
A typical load is 48–54 units (four to five subjects).

## Flexible P/NR (the current flexibility option)
For students who entered Fall 2020 or later, the old **Sophomore Exploratory** and **Junior/Senior P/D/F** options were replaced by **Flexible P/NR**:
- After your first semester, you get a budget of **48 units** (roughly four subjects) usable any time through your degree.
- You designate a subject P/NR **retroactively — after seeing your final grade** — up to Add Date of your next enrolled semester. A/B/C becomes P; D/F becomes NR.
- Usable on **any subject**, including GIRs and major/minor requirements. The designation is irreversible once made.
- (Departments/programs may have their own rules about flexible grades — check before relying on one for a requirement.)

## Regular grading
- Letter grades: A, B, C, D, F, with internal +/- modifiers (on internal grade reports but **not** on the external transcript or in GPA).
- MIT GPA is on a **5.0 scale**: A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, F=0; GPA = Σ(units × points) / Σ units, rounded to one decimal.
- Excluded from GPA: P, S, and incomplete-type grades (I, OX, J, U).
- **Incomplete (I)** — for missing a small amount of work for good cause, arranged with the instructor. **Listener (L)** — auditing, no credit.

## Dropping
- **Add Date** (~5 weeks in) and **Drop Date** (~3/4 through the term) are generous — dropping by Drop Date leaves no record. After Drop Date, late drops require petitioning (S³ helps undergrads with CAP petitions).
- Registering for an extra subject and dropping later is normal and expected.

Check the Registrar's grading-policies page for the current year's exact rules — flexible-grading policies have changed over the years, and the details above are cohort-dependent.
