(Verified against the official PE, Recreation, and Athletics sites, Aug 2026.)

## PE classes (the GIR)
- 8 points needed (see the GIR topic). Registration is **first come, first served — not a lottery** — and popular classes (sailing, pistol, archery, figure skating) fill within minutes.
- **2026–27 undergrad registration openings: Dec 2, 2026, Jan 29, 2027, and Mar 3, 2027, each at 8:00am** (Q1 registration happens just before fall term; grad students get the final day of each window). Set an alarm.
- Quarters run ~5–6 weeks (Q1 from the first week of classes; Q3 mid-Feb to mid-March, as reference points). You must attend the first class to keep your spot.
- Categories: aquatics, martial arts, racquet sports, fitness/strength, dance, outdoor (sailing!), plus wellness classes.
- **Swim test**: offered during orientation and through the year via PE&W (physicaleducationandwellness.mit.edu/swim-test).

## Varsity (33 sports)
16 men's, 15 women's, 2 coed — one of the largest college programs; mostly Division III with DI rowing. Walk-ons are realistic in several sports (contact head coaches via the mitathletics.com staff directory), and two varsity seasons satisfy the PE requirement.

## Club sports (~34 teams, ~1,100 athletes)
Alpine skiing, archery, badminton, cheer, cricket, curling, cycling, figure skating, golf, gymnastics, ice hockey (M/W), judo and several martial arts, rowing, rugby (M/W), running club, soccer (M/W), sport pistol, sport taekwondo, squash, table tennis, tennis, triathlon, ultimate (M/W), volleyball. Full list: clubsports.mit.edu.

## Intramurals (~18–20 sports)
Low-commitment leagues, games about weekly for 6–8 weeks; dorms, FSILGs, and departments field teams. Captains register on IMLeagues via intramurals.mit.edu — typically early September (fall), late November (IAP), mid-February (spring). Mostly free. The **Beaver Cup** goes to the year's best overall IM community.

## Facilities and access
- All enrolled students get MIT Recreation access: fitness floors, open-rec swim, indoor and outdoor tracks. The **Z Center** (Olympic-size pool + instructional pool, two fitness floors, six squash courts, golf range) is the hub; also duPont, Rockwell Cage, Johnson, the ice rink, and outdoor fields.
- Typical hours: Z Center ~6am–11pm (live hours at mitrecsports.com).
- **Free court bookings** for students (weekday afternoons/evenings and weekends, up to 48h ahead). Group-exercise classes are bundled into membership.
- **Sailing is free**: a sailing card comes with student athletic privileges; Learn to Sail runs weekday evenings April–September at the Sailing Pavilion.

## getfit
The 8-week team fitness challenge (teams of 4–8, whole MIT community) each spring — the 2026 edition ran Feb 9 – Apr 5.
