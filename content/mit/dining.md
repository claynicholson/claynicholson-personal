## Dining halls and meal plans
House dining halls: **Maseeh (Howard Dining Hall — the largest, serving all meal periods)**, **Baker**, **Next House**, **Simmons**, and **New Vassar** (McCormick's is closed with the dorm's 2026–28 renovation). All-you-care-to-eat swipes. Typical hours: breakfast 8–11, lunch 11–3, dinner 5–9, plus **late night Sun–Thu 10pm–1am**.

Students living in dining-hall dorms must enroll in a meal plan. Six options: **Any 21** (a swipe every meal period, every day) and **Block plans** — 225, 190, 160, 90, 60 meals/semester. 2025–26 prices ran from ~$1,174 (Block 60) to ~$3,824 (Any 21 / Block 225) per semester; door rates were ~$10.85 breakfast / $17 lunch / $19.95 dinner. Cook-for-yourself dorm residents can optionally buy any plan.

## Retail dining and cafés
Campus cafés and quick options cluster in the Student Center (W20), Stata, and the Sloan/Kendall side, plus food trucks. **TechCASH** (stored value on your MIT ID) works at campus dining, many nearby restaurants, laundry, and printing.

## Cook-for-yourself life
EC/Random/Burton Conner/MacGregor/New House culture includes floor and hall cooking. Groceries: Trader Joe's and Whole Foods within reach, H Mart in Central Square — and MIT runs **free weekend grocery shuttles** (Trader Joe's/Whole Foods Fridays + alternating Sundays; Market Basket/Star Market Saturdays; Costco/Target/Aldi Sundays — see the transportation topic).

## The free food ecosystem
- The **free-food mailing list** ("psst, free food in 32-G882") — legendary; join it.
- Club meetings, seminars, and sponsor events feed a large fraction of campus.
- Food security: **SwipeShare** (donated guest swipes), **TechMart** (at-cost grocery store in Walker Memorial), dorm food pantries, and the resource hubs at doingwell.mit.edu/foodandfinancial and advising.mit.edu/food-resource-guide — use them; money for food should never be the thing that breaks a semester.

## Nearby food (the classics)
Kendall Square (fancier lunch), Central Square (cheap and diverse), Flour Bakery and Area Four toward west campus, Chinatown and Allston via the T for group dinners. Late-night delivery culture is strong; every dorm has standing recommendations.
