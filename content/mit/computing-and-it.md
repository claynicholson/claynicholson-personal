(Specifics checked against ist.mit.edu, Aug 2026.)

## Your accounts
- **Kerberos identity** (username@mit.edu) — created before arrival; it's your login for everything. Choose the username carefully; it's ~permanent.
- **Touchstone** — MIT's single sign-on; **Duo** two-factor is mandatory.
- MIT email runs on Microsoft 365 (office.com with Touchstone); Google Workspace is also provided.
- **MIT ID card** — building access, TechCASH, library borrowing.

## Free software (don't pay before checking ist.mit.edu)
Free to students: **Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Adobe Creative Cloud, MATLAB** (including on personal machines), **Mathematica, SolidWorks, Dropbox, Zoom, Slack, PyMOL, SnapGene**, and more. VPN is **GlobalProtect** (Prisma Access) for off-campus access to licensed resources. Add the GitHub Student Pack etc. on top.

## Networks
**MIT SECURE** wifi everywhere on campus (Kerberos login); **eduroam** works here and at other universities worldwide; MIT GUEST for visitors; wired ports in dorms.

## Printing
- **Pharos** gives everyone a **$300/year allowance (~3,000 B&W or ~1,500 color pages), resetting each June** — registered students can exceed it. Send jobs via print.mit.edu (Athena Print Center), release at any Pharos printer, check usage at techcash.mit.edu.
- **CopyTech** (main: 11-004; east: E52-076) for large-format color posters up to 60" wide (the standard spot for UROP poster sessions), binding, and print services — order at printservices.mit.edu.

## Athena
MIT's campus computing environment: Linux cluster workstations across campus, `athena.dialup.mit.edu` for SSH, and your **Athena locker** (AFS storage + simple web hosting at web.mit.edu/username).

## Help
- **IS&T Service Desk**: walk-in at the **Atlas Service Center (E17-106)**, M–F 8–5; 617-253-1101; servicedesk@mit.edu; self-serve answers at the Knowledge Base (kb.mit.edu). Computer repair at the Hardware Service Center (56-129).
- Student-run: dorm computing chairs, and **SIPB** (Student Information Processing Board, W20-557) — the student computing group behind scripts.mit.edu, XVM hosting, and decades of campus infrastructure; visit for hosting/nerd help.
