**UROP (Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program)** is MIT's flagship program for undergrads doing real research with faculty — the large majority of undergrads do at least one.

## The basics
- Open to all undergrads from freshman fall onward. No GPA cutoff.
- You work in a lab/group under a faculty supervisor (often day-to-day with a grad student or postdoc).
- Available fall, spring, IAP, and summer.

## Pay, credit, or volunteer
Each term you choose one mode:
- **Pay**: hourly wage (rate set by UROP; check urop.mit.edu for the current rate). Funding either from **UROP direct funding** (you apply; competitive-ish but commonly granted) or **supervisor funding** (the lab pays).
- **Credit**: units toward your record (counts in your term load, graded P/D/F or letter depending on department).
- **Volunteer**: allowed but generally discouraged when funding is available.

## Finding a UROP
1. Browse openings on the UROP website's listings, but know most UROPs come from **cold-emailing professors** whose work interests you (short, specific email + why them + your availability; attach resume). Response rates are decent — follow up once.
2. Ask in classes/advising; departments and labs often advertise.
3. First-years: totally normal to start with zero experience — say what you want to learn.

## Applications and deadlines
- Every UROP requires a short **proposal** (project description written with your supervisor) submitted each term by the UROP deadline — deadlines are strict, especially for **direct funding** (typically early in the prior term for summer; check the site).
- Summer UROPs are the biggest commitment (full-time, paid) and a common alternative to an internship, especially early on.

## Related programs
- **SuperUROP**: year-long advanced research program (mainly EECS and some other departments) with a seminar and stipend/scholarship title.
- **UROP abroad / MISTI**: research placements internationally (see the research-programs topic).
- **ELO (Experiential Learning Opportunities)** umbrella covers UROP plus other hands-on programs.
