**UROP (Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program)** is MIT's flagship program for undergrads doing real research with faculty — the large majority of undergrads do at least one. (Specifics checked against urop.mit.edu, Aug 2026.)

## The basics
- Open to all undergrads from freshman fall onward. No GPA cutoff.
- You work in a lab/group under a faculty supervisor (often day-to-day with a grad student or postdoc).
- Available fall, spring, IAP, and summer.

## Pay, credit, or volunteer
Each term you choose one mode:
- **Pay**: **$16.25/hour** (AY 2026–27 rate). Funding comes either from **UROP direct funding** (central UROP office funds — you apply by the early deadline; caps: **$2,440/term (150 hrs) in fall/spring, $7,800 (480 hrs) for summer**) or **supervisor/DLC funding** (the lab's research funds pay).
- **Credit**: units toward your record (counts in your term credit limit).
- **Volunteer**: allowed but generally the last resort when funding exists.

## Deadlines (they're strict — direct funding especially)
Applications go through the online UROP system by 11:59 pm ET, then your supervisor approves:
- **Fall 2026**: direct funding — **Sept 15, 2026** (mentor approval Sept 17); supervisor-funded / for-credit — **Oct 20, 2026** (approval Oct 26).
- **IAP 2027**: special first-time-first-year direct funding — **Dec 1, 2026**; Wellesley cross-reg — Nov 13, 2026.
- The pattern: direct-funding deadlines fall ~1–2 weeks into each term (spring 2026's was Feb 3); supervisor/credit deadlines ~5 weeks in. Check urop.mit.edu/deadlines for each term's exact dates.

## Finding a UROP
1. Browse openings on urop.mit.edu, but know most UROPs come from **cold-emailing professors** whose work interests you (short, specific email + why them + your availability; attach resume). Response rates are decent — follow up once.
2. Ask in classes/advising; departments and labs advertise on mailing lists.
3. First-years: totally normal to start with zero experience — say what you want to learn.
4. Every UROP needs a short **proposal** (project description written with your supervisor) submitted each term.

## Related programs
- **SuperUROP**: yearlong advanced research program administered by EECS, open to School of Engineering and SHASS students — two-semester 6.UAR seminar for credit plus a monthly stipend (superurop.mit.edu).
- **MISTI**: funded international research/internships (see the research-programs topic).
- Summer UROPs are full-time, paid, and a common early alternative to an industry internship.
