## Campus shuttles (free)
- **Tech Shuttle** — daytime loop around campus (west dorms ↔ Kendall).
- **SafeRide** — evening/night shuttle routes covering campus + FSILG neighborhoods in Boston/Cambridge; the safe way home late.
- **Boston Daytime Shuttle** and grocery shuttles (weekend runs to Trader Joe's/Whole Foods areas) — schedules on the transportation site; live tracking via the shuttle-tracking app the Institute currently uses.
- **EZRide** — Cambridgeport/North Station shuttle MIT participates in.

## MBTA (the T)
Campus sits on the **Red Line** (Kendall/MIT station); Central Square is a 10-minute walk from west campus. MIT sells **semester T passes at a substantial discount** through the transportation office (order before the term starts). Buses 1 and CT1 run down Mass Ave through campus.

## Bikes
- **Bluebikes** (regional bikeshare) — discounted membership for MIT affiliates; stations all over campus.
- Bike registration, racks everywhere, indoor dorm bike rooms; take bike theft seriously (U-lock).
- Charles River paths make Boston genuinely bikeable; winter biking is a lifestyle choice.

## Airport and regional
- **Logan Airport**: Red Line → Silver Line (free from airport), or ~20-min rideshare; peak break days, dorms organize shared rides.
- **South Station** (Amtrak/buses to NYC) is two Red Line stops away.
- **Zipcar** car-share on campus for grocery/Costco/IKEA runs; discounted student membership.

## Parking
Student parking permits exist but are expensive and limited — most undergrads don't keep cars. Visitor parking is scarce; garages in Kendall are pricey.

## Walking reality
Campus is a mile long; the **Infinite Corridor** shelters the main east-west walk. Winter is real: get boots, use the tunnels (the basement tunnel network connects many main-campus buildings — perfectly legitimate to walk through).
