## Everyday vocabulary
- **pset** — problem set; the unit of MIT life ("psetting" = doing them, usually in groups, often late)
- **hosed** — overwhelmed with work ("I'm so hosed this week"); from "drinking from a firehose"
- **tool** (v.) — to study/work hard; **tooling** all night
- **punt** (v.) — to skip/blow off work ("punted the reading"); punt vs. tool is the eternal tension
- **hack** — (1) a clever prank (police car on the Dome), (2) exploring campus (roof-and-tunnel hacking); hacker ethics are folklore-canon
- **IHTFP** — the unofficial motto; officially "I Have Truly Found Paradise," universally "I Hate This F***ing Place" — both sincerely, simultaneously
- **Course X** — your major (Course 6 = EECS); **subject** = a class; classes go by number ("I'm taking 18.06")
- **GIRs** — General Institute Requirements; also loosely = the science core
- **UROP** — research program/position ("my UROP"); rhymes with "you-rop"
- **IAP** — January term; **REX** — dorm exploration at orientation; **CPW** — admitted students weekend
- **P/NR** — first-semester Pass/No Record; **ASE** — advanced standing exam
- **HASS** — humanities requirement classes (HASS-H/A/S); **CI-H/CI-M** — communication-intensive subjects
- **the Infinite** — the Infinite Corridor; **the Dome** — Building 10's Great Dome; **the Charles** — the river
- **Athena** — campus computing; **Kerberos/kerb** — your MIT username; **zephyr** — ancient chat protocol (mostly historical); **dormspam** — mass emails to dorm lists advertising events (with `bcc'd to dorms` etiquette footers)
- **TechCASH** — ID-card money; **The Coop** — the bookstore (rhymes with "loop")
- **Brass Rat** — the MIT class ring (beaver mascot; flipped at graduation)
- **Tim the Beaver** — mascot ("nature's engineer"); **Tim Tickets** — visitor/event ticketing system
- **East vs. West campus** — the cultural axis (DIY/counterculture east; social/athletic-ish west; endlessly debated, half-true)
- **FSILG** — fraternities, sororities, independent living groups; **ILG** — the independent ones
- **S³ ("S-cubed")** — Student Support Services; **CAP** — Committee on Academic Performance; **COD** — Committee on Discipline
- **SIPB** — student computing group; **ASA** — club umbrella org; **UA** — Undergraduate Association (student gov)
- **MISTI** — funded international programs; **GEL** — Gordon Engineering Leadership program
- **2.009-style number-speak** — everything is numbers: buildings ("meet at 26-100"), majors, classes, even food trucks get coordinates

## Reading class numbers
`6.1010` = Course 6 subject; historically 6.009 etc. — EECS renumbered subjects in 2022 (6.0001→6.100A, 6.006 stayed), so old-timers and OCW use old numbers; both float around. `18.06` = linear algebra (legendary), `8.01/8.02` = physics core, `2.009` = MechE's famous product design capstone (now evolved), `6.100A` = intro Python.

## Sayings
- "Getting an education from MIT is like taking a drink from a fire hose." — the founding metaphor
- "Sleep, grades, social life: pick two." — the classic triangle (rejecting it is a rite of passage)
- "Mens et Manus" — the actual motto: mind and hand.
