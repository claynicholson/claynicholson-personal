### ASA recognition & Midway
- The ASA (Association of Student Activities) recognizes new student groups via a New Group Application; the Spring '26 cycle was due 23:59 on 20 March 2026. The process usually takes at least several weeks and can take a few months; the ASA primarily looks for a unique purpose and anticipated use of resources. ### UA Finboard
- The UA Financial Board (Finboard) funds ASA-recognized student groups that are majority-undergraduate; it allocates over $100,000 every semester. Four funding cycles per year: Winter (Jan 1–Mar 31), Spring (Apr 1–Jun 30), Summer (Jul 1–Sep 30), Fall (Oct 1–Dec 31) — https://ua.mit.edu/financial-board — VERIFIED

### GSC Funding Board
- The GSC Funding Board is the main funding body for ASA-recognized, majority-graduate student groups.
